What the sweep decided

Small Blocks do not pay, and AI does not run on flare wells.

Size: 4.8 MW, not 1 MW

24/7 staff, protected fiber and the N+1 spare genset cost the same at any Block size. Payback is 50 years at 0.6 MW, 18 years at 1.2 MW and 7.6 years at 4.8 MW. Cost per kW of IT halves from 1.2 to 4.8 MW.

Fuel: firm gas, not flare gas

A 300 mcf/d well powers only a 1.2 MW Block, and it loses more than half its gas in year one. AI tenants expect Tier III. The Block buys 1.1 MMcf/d under a firm contract at a processing plant or gathering header. That is under 0.5% of one 275 MMcf/d plant.

The flare-gas mining Block in deliverables/study.md stays a separate product. Its "phase 3 hybrid" is replaced by this one.

Recips beat turbines: $2,550/kW vs $5,200/kW at 2 MW (DOE), and turbines lose about 20% at 100 °F. Fast-response recips take 100% block load in one step; a typical lean-burn set takes 30%. Dry-only saves one genset and $3.6M but throttles GPUs on about 24 afternoons above 100 °F. Scenario 10 is the fallback.

Chosen configuration · scenario 9

What sits inside the fence.

Power island

6 × Cat G3516 Fast Response class, 1,500 ekW continuous, derated to 1,200 kW on field gas. 5 run, 1 spare. NOx 0.5 g/bhp-hr without aftertreatment, half the federal limit.

Electrical

Two 480 V buses (G1–G3, G4–G6) with a closed tie, each under the ~8 MW low-voltage limit. No medium-voltage gear and no transformer queue on the generation side.

Battery

2 × 600 kW / 600 kWh, one per bus. It covers a genset trip, fast GPU swings and checkpoint dips. GB300 shelves hold only 65 J per GPU.

Cooling

3 × 2.3 MW CDUs (2+1), 3 × 3 MW dry coolers rated to 55 °C ambient (2+1), packaged DX for 480 kW of air heat. PUE 1.11 annual, 1.15 peak. Zero operating water.

IT

4 halls, one scalable unit each (8 racks, 1.2 MW). Benchmark: Vertiv's 1.3 MW MegaMod in 801 sq ft. Racks up to 3,300 lb.

Network

A/B fiber laterals to the I-20 carrier route and a protected 100G wave. Latency is about 1.6 ms round trip per 100 route-miles, against a 2 s first-token budget.

Plot plan · to scale

92 × 56 m, about 1.3 acres.

GasGensets (12.2 × 3.0 m)SwitchgearBatteryIT hallsCoolingNetwork

Spacing: engines at least 5 ft from openings (NFPA 37); battery at least 10 ft from everything (IFC 2024 / NFPA 855). Noise about 50 dB(A) at 90 m, against the Texas 85 dB nuisance yardstick. One 1.77 MW Armada unit alone takes 0.12 acre.

Single-line · simplified

Two 480 V buses, a closed tie and a battery on each side.

MCC = motor control centre for CDUs, dry coolers and DX. Full A and B dual feed to every hall, which Tier III's "no single point of failure" implies, is a FEED-stage option to price. Plan halls for an 800 V DC retrofit from 2027.

  3. Battery1–2 quartersMegapack quoted Q1 2027 delivery in late Sept 2026.
  4. Modular IT halls12 weeks–3 monthsArmada and Crusoe Spark vendor claims.
  5. EGU Standard Permit9 days avg (2024)West of I-35/I-37 only. East Texas needs SCR at 0.14 lb/MWh.

Permit pause risk

On Sept 21, 2026 the governor directed TCEQ to halt all permits sought by data centers. TCEQ applied it to every air and water authorization for infrastructure directly supporting data centers, with no size threshold. It was lifted Nov 13 and review resumed Nov 17. The pause was never codified, so it can come back. File early, and have counsel review holding the permit in the gas or power company's name.

Research begins here

Ten checks, ordered by how much they move the decision.

  1. Hall cost. Budget quotes from Vertiv MegaMod, Armada, Crusoe Spark, Giga. Moves payback 5.7–9.3 years.
  2. Tenant and rent. Who leases 4.8 MW islanded, at what rent and SLA? Crusoe's islanded microgrid reached 99.2%.
  3. Firm gas. Terms for 1.1 MMcf/d at a plant tailgate or gathering header. Waha congestion may return in 2028.
  4. Transient study. Can recips follow 0.2–3 Hz swings in island mode? That sets the battery size (Williams 46% vs VoltaGrid none).
  5. Gas analysis. Chromatograph on the candidate supply; G3516 FR derate at the measured methane number.
  1. Dry-only summer. Dry-cooler approach at 40–47 °C ambient, and hours of throttling.
  2. Fiber. FiberLight and Segra quotes for laterals and a protected 100G wave on Permian routes.
  3. Permit text. Confirm the EGU Standard Permit PDF; watch for the pause returning.
  4. Staffing. Remote monitoring plus on-call instead of 24/7 onsite staff. That could make a 1.2 MW Block viable.
  5. 800 V DC. Design halls to retrofit 800 V DC power for 2027+ racks.
