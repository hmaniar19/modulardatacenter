The pivot

They started selling AI software and ended up selling stamped engineering.

  1. Sep 2025$5.5M seed recorded (Indexed). Product: AI agents for EPC bidding. They read every spec, P&ID and vendor sheet, then draft RFIs, material takeoffs and bid evaluations.
  2. Jan 2026SE Ventures invests. Pitch: an "intelligence layer" for EPCs. Bidding costs $1M and 12 weeks; Candid claims 10x faster, error from ±25% down to ±10%.
  3. Feb 2026$6M seed closes (PitchBook). Presight-Shorooq (G42) joins.
  4. Apr–Jun 2026All 7 job posts are delivery engineers and sales: process, piping, mechanical, electrical, VP Projects. Posted at $100K–$250K. No AI researchers.
  5. Nov 2026"We are an owner's engineering firm for gas-fired power. You work with engineers, not a software tool."

The lesson: selling AI tools to big contractors is slow. Selling a familiar product (stamped design packages) that AI makes cheap to produce is faster.

How they consult

Four steps from "we have a site" to "ready to commit capital".

Intake

One basis of design, three candidate sites. Gaps are filled from satellite imagery, public records and nearby plants. Assumptions are marked.

Scenario sweep

Software runs 12 scenarios across sites and seasons in industry-standard tools. It draws on 350+ GW of plant engineering.

Live pricing

Rolling vendor quotes and channel partners keep cost and schedule current. Client quotes are used under NDA.

PE stamp

A licensed engineer in the project's state reviews and signs every sheet. Delivered in the client's own templates.

Deliverables

Plot plan, heat and material balance, single-line, PFD to P&ID, cost estimate, schedule, scope gaps, long-lead risk.

Scope and speed

Site and configuration studies, interconnection and permitting support, pre-FEED in 4 weeks, FEED in 12, then handoff to the EPC. "We don't build."

How they sell

A new firm beats Tier 1 firms by taking the risk away from the buyer.

Free blind backtest

"Hand us a plant you already built." They estimate it blind, and the client compares the result to actual cost.

Late means free

A package delivered late is not paid for.

Warranted rework

When the project or prices change, the package is updated at no charge.

Founder-led demo

A 30-minute live session. Replies come from a founder's inbox within a business day.

Outbound engine

RB2B identifies website visitors for follow-up (per their privacy policy). EPC Show panel. Greentown Houston.

Enterprise trust

SOC 2 Type I, ISO 27001 aligned, an NDA on every engagement, encryption.

Our think tank

A separate department with its own engineering, permitting and cost procedures.

It is not part of the data center business's operating procedures. It has its own name, QA and stamping procedure, books and data room.

Separate LLC

Engineering liability stays outside the data center operation. About $300 to form, and reversible.

Information barrier

Outside clients' sites, gas positions and costs never reach the parent. Internal work uses the same intake, contract and pricing as outside work.

Wedge

1–100 MW BTM gas power for data centers and modular compute: recip engines, aeroderivatives, small turbines, stranded and flare-gas sites. Candid's showcase is a $108M combined-cycle plant. We go smaller and faster.

Full scope

Every gate Candid covers, from site screen to EPC handoff.

1 · Site screen (FEL1)

Gas supply, grid or islanded, land, water, air-permit class, fiber. Go or no-go.

2 · Configuration (FEL2)

Prime mover, N+x redundancy, heat balance, cooling, scenario sweep.

3 · Engineering

Plot plan, PFD, P&ID, single-line, heat and material balance, equipment list, MTO.

4 · Cost and schedule

Class 4/3 estimate, long-lead tracker, vendor quote book.

5 · Permitting and interconnection

TCEQ air (standard permit or PBR), water, land use, ERCOT large-load or islanded study inputs.

6 · Owner's engineer

Lender independent-engineer responses, bid evaluation of EPC offers, through FID.

7 · EPC handoff

Native file formats, so nothing is redrawn.

8 · AI layer

Document ingestion, requirement extraction, cross-discipline checks, change propagation, backtest library.

Why lean

cheaper to run until demand is proven.

A Candid-sized team of 14 at their posted $100k–$250k band costs about $3.2M a year in payroll inferred. Our lean department costs about $207k a year in fixed costs, and adds engineers only when the delivery load requires them.

First 90 days

License, prove accuracy, then sell.

  1. Weeks 0–2Form the LLC. Sign the part-time Texas PE. File TBPELS registration (issuance takes 5–6 weeks). Bind professional liability. Write the MSA with capped warranty and late-delivery terms.
  2. Weeks 2–6Build the backtest library. Rebuild 5–10 public small gas or BTM plants from permits, EIA filings and vendor sheets. Publish one sample pack.
  3. Weeks 4–12Offer 3 free blind backtests to developers and convert them to paid site screens. Founder-led demos. One Houston power or data center event.
  4. On signed demandBuy SOC 2 and Thermoflow only when a signed client requires them. Hire the first engineer when load exceeds about 2 pre-FEED packs a month.
Risks and next steps

What could break it, and what to check first.

Not yet proven

  • Demand and price. No buyer has been asked. The sales ramp and prices are estimates, and every result above depends on them.
  • Delivery capacity. How many packs two founders can deliver with AI tooling is untested. It sets the first-hire date.
  • Insurance. A real quote may exceed the $10k midpoint once power-plant design exposure is disclosed.
  • Candid unknowns. Prices, revenue and customers are undisclosed. The valuation and the Enverus figure (data centers are 88% of forecast BTM demand) are search snippets only.

Next actions

  1. 10 discovery calls with BTM and data center developers. Test the $12k and $55k price points.
  2. Run 3 free blind backtests and measure paid conversion.
  3. Shortlist a part-time Texas PE and get a professional-liability quote.
  4. Put the real numbers into docs/research/candid_lean_model.py and rerun it.
