# Owner brief (fixed once locked)

Goal: decide whether, and how, to build a drop-and-go modular data center that runs on
stranded or flared natural gas at oil and gas wellsites, the way Texas bitcoin miners
use flare gas today. Analyze it in phases and deeply enough to act on.

The owner's asks, each must be answered in deliverables/study.md:
1. A modular data center (containerized or trailer-mounted, drop and go).
2. Fuel: flared or trapped (stranded) natural gas, like bitcoin flare-gas mining in Texas.
3. A SWOT analysis.
4. First-principles building (the SpaceX approach): retrofit or repurpose parts where
   practical, and have a practical path when equipment is backlogged.
5. Requirements: what latency and power the site needs. Minimum gas supply is 300 mcf/d
   per well or tie-in group. Recommend the prime mover (recip engine, turbine, or other)
   from research.
6. If the only real need is fiber or decent internet, can a modular trailer be brought in
   to retrofit stranded gas wells, and what connectivity works there.
7. Cooling: consider a closed loop cycle, but it must make sense on capex and opex.
8. Water: can DAF units be outfitted for local SWD plants, use potable water, or have
   water trucks bring water instead of going to SWD plants; is a conversion kit
   available; is TCEQ (or other agency) approval a nightmare; is temporary use simpler;
   other industrial engineering or business practices that reduce opex.

Defaults (owner did not specify; the study may argue against them with evidence):
- Geography: Texas first (Permian and Eagle Ford), other basins as comparison only.
- Workload: compare bitcoin mining, AI or GPU compute, and a hybrid. Pick one for site 1.
- Budget: not fixed. Show capex for one site at the 300 mcf/d minimum.
- Be practical: name real vendors, real lead times, and what to do when parts are backlogged.
